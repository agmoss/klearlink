title: Positive Payment Compliance
---

**Problem**

Lenders have limited tools to view positive (or negative) payment compliance with other lenders at the time of the first credit decision and as borrowing cycles continue.

**Profile**

- New customer applies for the first time online for a payday loan
- The credit score is poor, as expected, at 430. Significant delinquencies on old types of credit that are 12-24 months old. No credit has been reported on the bureau since prior defaults.
- A Clearlinc match is found.
- The matched customer has two active payday loans.
  - Both Payday loans have 100% positive payment compliance since inception 3 and 6 months ago 1

**Solution**

The payday lender checks for a consumer match with Clearlinc to get a clear picture of the consumers payment compliance.

**Outcome**

The lender knows that they are payday loan #3 in the credit stack and that the customer is a strong performer on other payday loans despite poor credit history.
