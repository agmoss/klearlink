# Term Glossary

- **Consumer Credit Record**: The assembly of Credit Facts and Consumer Facts. A consumer credit record is the input into the KlearLink system.
  - **Credit Facts**: Information on the consumers' credit they have or applied to have from a lending organization.
  - **Consumer Facts**: Information on the loan applicant (consumer).
- **Credit State**: The lifecycle state of the borrower's individual credit product. (part of Credit Facts)
- **Consumer Match**: The relationship between a Consumer Credit Record submitted by your organization and a Consumer Credit Record submitted by a different organization. A consumer match is the outcome of the KlearLink system.

- **API**: Software interface for submitting lending instances and obtaining lending matches. The API system allows lenders to provide lending instances and consume consumer matches.

- **Webhooks**: Software interface for real-time updates for lending matches.
- **Data ETL**: An alternative to API Steps 1 & 2 that automates the submission of lending instances from a lender's org into the KlearLink system.
